      Sources and method

        Annual figures are calendar-year averages of the monthly, weekly or quarterly data. "Today's dollars" means dollars, adjusted with CPI-U. Home prices are the national median for houses sold, so they don't capture differences between cities. Income figures are pre-tax. Mortgage payments are principal and interest only. Rent between census years is an estimate built from the CPI rent index.

        Build or refresh the data with python3 scripts/build_data.py.
